- **Net downhill but deceptive:** Early downhills can trash quads if pace is too aggressive

- **Newton Hills (miles 16-21):** Four hills including Heartbreak; run by effort, not pace

- **Fueling:** Plan for gels at miles 5, 10, 15, 20

- **Weather:** April in Boston is variable; prepare for anything 35-65°F

  

### Achilles Management Race Week

  

- Continue eccentric heel drops through Wednesday of race week

- Light calf raises Monday/Tuesday only

- Ice if any discomfort

- Bring KT tape as backup

- Warm up thoroughly race morning (20-25 min easy jog + dynamic stretching)
